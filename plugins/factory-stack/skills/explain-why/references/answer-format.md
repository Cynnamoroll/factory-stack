# Answer format

Use these sections in this order. Keep the confidence tiers from [epistemics.md](epistemics.md) intact.

## The question
One or two sentences.

## The code in question
Paths, line ranges, and key symbols. Two or three lines for a cold reader.

## What we found
- **[Direct]** Claim. Source: PR #123, ticket ID, URL, or `file:line`. Short quote.
- **[Supported]** Claim. Evidence: each item and what it adds.

## What we can reasonably infer
- **[Inferred]** Hedged claim. Reasoning: the evidence and the inference step.

Skip when there is nothing to infer.

## Competing hypotheses
For each: the hypothesis, the evidence for it, and the evidence against or missing. Skip when one answer is clear.

## What we don't know
Specific gaps: the question, the sources searched, the queries, and what came back. Name people who would likely know.

## Sources consulted
One line per category: source control, issue tracker, long-form docs, team chat, observability, error tracking, analytics warehouse. For each, what was searched, or "not searched" with the reason.

## Confidence summary
One or two sentences on overall confidence, naming which parts are solid and which are open.
